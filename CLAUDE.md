# CLAUDE.md — Química Física II lecture notes (UPV/EHU)

## What this repository is

Lecture notes ("apuntes") for **Química Física II** (quantum chemistry, 3rd
year, UPV/EHU), by David De Sancho. LaTeX sources using the `tufte-handout`
class, one standalone document per topic. The course content itself is written
in **Spanish** — this instruction file is the only English document here.

Two remotes, currently at the same commit:

- `github` → `https://github.com/KimikaTeorikoa/QuimicaFisicaII_Handouts.git`
  (branch `main`; there is also `archive/overleaf-2023`)
- `origin` → `https://git@git.overleaf.com/64fae9a08351c9f6abbd5772` (branch
  `master`)

The local branch is `master`, tracking `origin/master`. **Overleaf is a live
editing target**: anything pushed to `origin` appears immediately in the project
the author may have open, and his own edits arrive as "Update on Overleaf."
commits. So: `git fetch` at the start of a session, and never push unless asked.

## How to build

```bash
make                # builds all 20 documents; stops at the first failure
make comprobar      # status table for all 20; does NOT abort, reports everything
make tema03         # a single document
make figuras        # regenerates figs/ by running the notebooks
make limpiar        # removes aux files (.aux .log .out .toc …)
make limpiar-todo   # also removes the PDFs
```

Variables: `PYTHON ?= $(HOME)/opt/anaconda3/bin/python3` (Homebrew's `python3`
has no scipy) and `LATEX ?= pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error`.
Everything is compiled **from the repository root**, including `ejercicios/`,
which is why the problem sheets' aux files land in the root.

To learn the state of the whole set always use `make comprobar`: plain `make`
stops at the first broken document and hides the rest.

## QF2 style conventions (derived from `tema01.tex`)

Preamble (`tema01.tex` 1–72): `\documentclass{tufte-handout}`,
`\usepackage[spanish, es-tabla]{babel}`, `amsmath`, `graphicx` with
`\setkeys{Gin}{width=\linewidth,totalheight=\textheight,keepaspectratio}`,
`booktabs`, `units`, `fancyvrb`, `multicol`. `tema01` adds what the others lack:
`\graphicspath{{figs/}{figs/retratos/}{graphics/}}` and
`\setlength{\@fptop}{0pt}` (top-aligns full-page floats, which otherwise centre
vertically and look like a mistake).

- **`\retrato{file}{caption text}`** (`tema01.tex` 67–72): historical portraits
  in the margin. It is a `marginfigure` **without `\caption`**, deliberately: it
  therefore consumes no figure number and does not shift existing `\ref`s. It
  carries a `[\baselineskip]` offset and a trailing `\vspace*{\baselineskip}` so
  the box reserves room and no sidenote can butt against the portrait. **The `%`
  after the `]` is not optional**: without it the image overflows the margin by
  2.33 pt. The author's LaTeX notes in the preamble (lines 46–65) explain all of
  this — keep them.
- After a `\retrato{}{}`, a `\begin{marginfigure}` or an `\end{figure}` placed
  mid-paragraph, close with `%` so no spurious space is injected.
- **Labels:** `eq:` for equations (`eq:Wien`, `eq:rayleigh-jeans`, `eq:planck`,
  `eq:work`, `eq:debroglie`, `eq:heisenberg`), `fig:` for figures (`fig:wien`,
  `fig:planck`, `fig:bohr`), `sec:` for sections. Descriptive names, never
  opaque abbreviations (the `eq:heiss` typo has already been fixed).
- **Cross-references in prose:** «Figura \ref{...}» and «Ecuación \ref{...}»,
  capitalised; inside a `\caption`, use `\protect\ref{...}`.
- **Float environments**, matched to the width the figure was generated at:
  `marginfigure` (2.0″), `figure` (4.2″), `figure*` (6.4″, full width).
  `\FloatBarrier` (from `placeins`, already loaded by `tufte-common.def`) flushes
  floats before a section.
- **Sidenotes:** `\sidenote{...}` is the vehicle for historical context, verbatim
  English quotations and Nobel prizes. `tema01` has 10 of them; unrevised topics
  barely 1. Using more of them is part of the house style.
- **Typography:** LaTeX quotes ``` ``…'' ```, never `"…"` nor `“…”`. Em dash
  `---` for parentheticals. Accents and ñ written **directly in UTF-8**
  (`condición`), never `\'o`; no `.tex` in the repo still uses escapes.
- **Units and numbers:** decimal point (`1.381\times 10^{-23}`), upright units
  with explicit exponents (`J K$^{-1}$`, `cm$^{-1}$`, `J$\cdot$s`); both `h` and
  `\hbar` in J·s. Textual subscripts in `\mathrm` (`\lambda_\mathrm{max}`,
  `E_\mathrm{cin}^{\max}`).
- **Notation the author has settled on:** uncertainty as
  $\Delta x\,\Delta p_x\ge\hbar/2$ (position and momentum **along the same
  direction**, and not presented as a limitation of the measuring apparatus); no
  $\Delta E\,\Delta t$ (explicit decision: time is not an observable); $\rho$ is
  the **spectral energy density**, not a density of states; **centripetal**
  force in the Bohr model.
- Key terms in `\textbf{}` on first appearance; English loanwords in
  `\textit{}` followed by lowercase «en inglés».
- The commented-out `thebibliography` and the stray `\bibliographystyle{plainnat}`
  that the other topics still carry are dead scaffolding; they were removed from
  `tema01`.

## Figure naming convention

```
<sistema>_<observable>[_<variante>]_v<N>.pdf
```

Real examples: `caja1d_psi-psi2_n1-4_v1.pdf`, `hidrogeno_rdf_1s-3d_v1.pdf`,
`diatomicas_diagrama-om_N2-O2_v1.pdf`,
`osciladorarmonico_limite-clasico_v20_v1.pdf`. Portraits follow
`retrato_<surname>_v1.jpg` under `figs/retratos/`.

`v<N>` is bumped when the data or the analysis change, **not** for cosmetic
tweaks; it is passed as `qf.save(..., v=2)`. The **vector PDF** is versioned;
the sibling PNG is for quick inspection only and is gitignored (`figs/*.png`).
Figures are **never drawn or edited by hand**: they always come from a notebook.

Portrait provenance lives in `figs/retratos/CREDITOS.md` (human-readable table)
and `creditos.json` (same content, machine-readable). All are public domain,
from Wikimedia Commons, rescaled to 600 px wide. If you add one, update both
files.

## Figure workflow

The notebooks in `notebooks/` are the **source of truth**; `figs/*.pdf` is
derived output.

- `make figuras` → `run_notebooks.py`, which executes each `nb*.ipynb` **in
  memory** via `nbclient`: the `.ipynb` on disk is not modified, so no outputs
  are ever committed. It forwards whatever the cells print and exits 1 if any
  cell fails.
- Single notebook: `python notebooks/run_notebooks.py nb05_hidrogeno.ipynb`.
- Notebook structure: an imports-and-constants cell, then one markdown title
  cell plus one code cell per figure, ending in `qf.save(...)`.
- `notebooks/qf2figs.py` is the shared style module: widths `qf.MARGIN=2.0`,
  `qf.TEXT=4.2`, `qf.FULL=6.4` (inches, calibrated for `tufte-handout`), a
  reordered Okabe-Ito palette validated for colour-vision deficiency, serif
  typography, `qf.figure()`, `qf.save()`, `qf.label_line()` (direct labels on
  curves **instead of a legend**) and `qf.zero_axis()`. `save()` sets
  `metadata={"CreationDate": None}` so regenerating does not dirty the diff.
- Several notebooks print **numerical checks** that act as regression tests
  ($r_{max}(2s)=5.24\,a_0$, oscillator variational 13.6 %, Hückel butadiene
  $\pm1.618,\pm0.618$, tetrahedral angle 109.47°…). If one of those numbers
  changes, something broke.
- Requires `numpy`, `scipy`, `matplotlib`, `nbformat`, `nbclient`.

## How a topic revision is done

1. `git fetch` from both remotes and read `CHECKPOINT.md` to see where work
   stopped.
2. Work **one topic at a time**, against its section of `REVIEW_PLAN.md §1`,
   verifying each finding against the current file (the plan's line numbers are
   stale).
3. Severity marks used throughout the plan:

   | Mark | Meaning |
   |---|---|
   | 🔴 | Blocker: breaks the build, or a physics/maths error the student will copy |
   | 🟠 | Real but non-blocking error (notation, a sign, a numerical value) |
   | 🟡 | Style, typography, grammar |
   | 🔵 | Pedagogical improvement or proposed new content |

4. **Order:** corrections first (🔴 → 🟠 → 🟡), improvements 🔵 afterwards. Do
   not mix a pedagogical rewrite into the fix for a typo.
5. Rebuild (`make temaNN`) and read the `.log`: zero errors and **zero overfull
   boxes** — `tema01` sits at 0 and that is the bar.
6. Update `REVIEW_PLAN.md` (mark resolved items ✅) and `CHECKPOINT.md` when
   closing the session.

### Language: the convention has two halves

- **All course content and its documentation in Spanish**: the `.tex` files,
  `REVIEW_PLAN.md`, `CHECKPOINT.md`, `notebooks/README.md`, `CREDITOS.md`, and
  the comments in the code and notebooks.
- **Commit messages in English**, imperative mood, prefixed `Tema NN:` when the
  change targets one topic, and stating the *why* rather than only the what.
  From the actual history: `Tema 01: fix the 2.33pt overfull the portrait
  padding introduced`, `Tema 01: close the gaps in the reasoning and tighten the
  wording`, `Tema 01: drop the dead bibliography scaffolding`, `Add figure
  infrastructure, Makefile and review plan`.

## Rules for the agent

- **Do not invent physics.** If a constant, a derivation or a statement does not
  add up, say so and ask; do not paper over the gap with a plausible version.
- **Do not change the author's pedagogical choices without asking** (what is
  included, in what order, with what notation). Some decisions are already made
  and recorded — $\Delta E\,\Delta t$ dropped, the Rayleigh portrait discarded —
  and are not to be reversed on your own initiative.
- **Do not renumber equations or figures** in ways that break `\ref`s: never
  reuse an existing label for something else, and check the `\ref`s if you move
  or delete anything labelled. This is exactly why `\retrato` carries no
  `\caption`.
- **Do not `git add`, `commit` or `push` unless asked.** `origin` is Overleaf,
  and a push changes what the author sees instantly.
- **Never edit generated files:** `*.aux`, `*.log`, `*.out`, the document PDFs,
  or `figs/*.pdf`. They are rebuilt by `make` / `make figuras`.
- **Do not touch the Tufte template files** (`tufte-*.cls`, `tufte-common.def`,
  `sample-handout.*`, `graphics/`, `History.txt`, `Manifest.txt`, `README.txt`)
  and do not treat them as course material.
- **Figures are regenerated, not retouched:** edit the notebook and run `make
  figuras`. Never open a PDF in `figs/` to correct it.
- **Prose stays in Spanish**, in the register of `tema01`: didactic first person
  plural ("veremos", "podemos escribir"), short sentences, no colloquialisms and
  no emoji in the notes themselves.
- **Compile before calling an edit done.** Nothing counts as finished until
  `make temaNN` reports OK.
